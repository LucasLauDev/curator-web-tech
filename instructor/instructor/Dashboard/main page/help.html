Common Questions

How do I upload a 4K video?

To maintain crystal-clear quality, upload MP4 files encoded with H.264 at 30–60 Mbps. Head to Course Builder, select 'Add Module', and drag your file into the uploader.

How do I manage student flags?

Flags appear in your Instructor Dashboard under the 'Students' tab. You can review flagged content, respond to the student, or escalate to our moderation team.

How to export student analytics?

Navigate to the 'Analytics' tab. Click 'Export Data' in the top-right corner and choose CSV, PDF, or XLSX format to download your student progress reports.

When are student payouts processed?

Payouts are typically processed on the 15th of every month for the previous month's activities. Ensure your details are verified in Settings at least 48 hours before the cycle.
