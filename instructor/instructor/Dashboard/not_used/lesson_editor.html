Micro-interactions are the silent ambassadors of your brand. They are the small, functional animations or feedback loops that happen when a user interacts with an interface. Think about the way a "Like" button heart pops or how a pull-to-refresh feels elastic.

In this lesson, we will explore the four parts of a micro-interaction:

  • Triggers: What initiates the micro-interaction.
  • Rules: What happens once it's triggered.
  • Feedback: How the user knows something happened.
The difference between a good product and a great one is the micro-interactions.
