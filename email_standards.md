# CuratorEdu Email Standards

To ensure academic integrity and consistent user identification, the following email domain standards are enforced across the platform:

## 1. Domain Requirements

| Role | Email Domain | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Students** | `@student.uts.edu.my` | `j.doe@student.uts.edu.my` |
| **Lecturers** | `@uts.edu.my` | `s.drasner@uts.edu.my` |
| **Administrators** | `@uts.edu.my` | `admin@uts.edu.my` |

## 2. Validation Rules

- All login and registration forms must validate the email suffix.
- Only the above two domains are accepted for account creation and invitation.
- Invitation codes are tied to the specific domain based on the selected role.
